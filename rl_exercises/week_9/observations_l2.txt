2.1 Hyperparameter Sensitivity
a.) The results are shown in "21a.png".
A planning horizon of 1 yields poor performance compared to the other configurations. Indicating that larger planning horizons are beneficial.
This trend continues for a planning horizon of 3. However, the margin of improvement (h=3 -> h=5) becomes much smaller compared to h=1 -> h=3.
The configurations with planning horizons larger than 5 (h=10, h=20) are performing worse than the configuration with h=5.

In conclusion, the optimal planning horizon is 5. Larger planning horizons are beneficial to a certain point. Afterward, the performance degrades.

b.) The results are shown in "21b.png".
The balanced configuration yields the optimal performance using the smallest number of real environment steps.
The aggressive configuration is a tiny bit worse than the balanced configuration in terms of overall IQM reward achieved.
The conservative configuration performs worst in this comparison.

c.) The results are shown in "21c.png".
The buffer size does not affect the performance of the agent too much. All configurations are performing similarly.
The only exception is the configuration with buffer size 1000. Its variance over the seeds is the highest of all configurations.
This indicates that the buffer is too small to represent the true distribution of the environment.
