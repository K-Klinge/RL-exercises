The addition of random network distillation for PPO leads to a large drop in performance in the LunarLander environment, and the variance
of the IQM returns is comparable in the two models.
When using DQN as the backbone model, the performance of the RND and non-RND models are comparable. However, the variance
of the IQM return of the RND model is much smaller than that of the non-RND model.
